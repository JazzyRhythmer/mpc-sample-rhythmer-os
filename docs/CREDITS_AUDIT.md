# Credits and source retention audit

The selected beta.8 native inventory contains 18 accepted ports: 13 FM-1/DRUM-X variants and five Move modules. The current source-to-stage comparison covers 221 named legal/credit documents with **zero missing or changed files**, and 556 SPDX-bearing source files. Sophie and the runtime retain their separate original MIT/AGPL notices; five unchanged drum packages retain their source pins and GPL notices.

Original predecessor authors are preserved through source headers, SOURCE.json, UPSTREAM_NOTICES/PROVENANCE.json and per-plugin third_party documents. The exact five Move source trees and populated submodules accompany MOVE-SOURCE; selected FM-1 units, adapters, runtime, JUCE and font sources accompany SOURCE. Reused MPC package sources are pinned by tag/commit in baseline-source.lock.json and its exported index. [FM-1 lineage](FM1_LINEAGE_KO.md) distinguishes full-firmware reference context from synthesis units actually included.

Original factory banks are retained under their source notices rather than replaced with Rhythmer recipes. Prophet's Sequential SysEx is separately attributed data, not relicensed by the software GPL. Elixir's factory/Hrtl banks carry the original Erica Synths permission notice for Elixir; this audit does not extend that permission to arbitrary standalone redistribution. Dexed uses its original 33 published banks. CS-1 includes the complete original tone and converted sound tables. See ORIGINAL_PRESET_AUDIT_KO.md for exact scope and exclusions. Hortator VCSL recordings retain CC0 attribution; reSID, Mutable Instruments and other dependency notices remain attached where used.

This is a checked source/notice inventory, not a blanket legal clearance: license_audit_complete remains false. The official MPC Sample device wordmark has separate trademark/asset terms in assets/device/NOTICE.md. Real-device validation and public distribution remain pending. Rhythmer OS is based on Melodee by Kerem Kiliç (Ellic Studio), without upstream or manufacturer endorsement.

The Break performance addition pins the unmodified FM-1 Rhythmer jungle.c, jungle.h and six_memory.h, complete Amenity licence and accompanying notices under third_party/fm1-break. The ancestry is Presethead Jungle Choppa, Amenity/FM1 Open, and the Rhythmer adaptation. The MPC reader/state/controller modifications are identified in NOTICE.md; no proprietary SDK or source break recording is imported.
