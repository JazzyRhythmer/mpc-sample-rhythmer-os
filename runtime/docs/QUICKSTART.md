# Rhythmer OS quickstart

Load a sample kit or choose one of the 20 instruments from PLUG-INS. Rhythmer Kit has 36 complete kits; other instruments play one sound chromatically or in scale.

Press SEQ for PATTERNS. Hold SHIFT and press B3 GEN. Select GENERATE SONG to assign a kit and three instruments to tracks 1–4, 5–8, 9–12 or 13–16. GEN SETUP changes the target or chooses MIDI-only tracks. GENERATE TRACK keeps the selected instrument. Turn the seed to reproduce a pattern. BACK returns from Acid/Break to GEN, then PATTERNS.

EDIT opens the engine main page. B3 edits the sound or selected kit pad. The wheel moves functional pages; PAGES shows a map. SHIFT+B3 toggles plug-in TRANSPORT. Double-tap STOP cuts all audio.

New plug-ins go in Rhythmer/Plugins on the card. Existing top-level Plugins remain readable. Use the device page or Files over USB at http://rhythmer.local. Firmware updates must be signed Rhythmer OS releases.

Based on Melodee by Kerem Kiliç (Ellic Studio). Developed by jazzy rhythmer.
